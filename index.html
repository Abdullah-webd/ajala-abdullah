AI Agent

RelayFlow

One AI agent across every business messaging channel. RelayFlow connects a business's WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack, and Gmail, then lets the owner ask what's been said, summarize any channel, and send one message everywhere — with a mandatory approve-before-send step so nothing goes out unreviewed.

Built for SMEs drowning in customer messages across four apps — now shipped under Levi AI at userelayflow.com, with an approve-before-send guardrail so nothing goes out unreviewed.
  • Connect flows for WhatsApp (QR), Telegram (phone/code/2FA), Slack and Gmail (OAuth).
  • Streaming agent with a live activity feed; ask about recent conversations on any channel.
  • Draft → approve → send, including one-shot broadcast to all connected channels.
  • Scheduled tasks that run an instruction on a cron and email you the result.
  • Eval harness: golden-set answers scored by an LLM judge plus scope-drift and prompt-injection tests — guardrails added from what it found.

AI Fraud Detection

TrustBridge AI

Real-time fraud protection for bank customers — 2nd place at Wema Hackaholics 7.0. Where KYC checks identity once at signup, TrustBridge's "PYC" (Protect Your Customer) engine scores every login and transfer against the customer's own behavioural baseline and intervenes during the attack, not after the money is gone.

Built for bank customers and fraud desks — presented live to Wema Bank judges; designed to sit behind a bank as an SDK.
  • Behavioural risk engine with a graduated ladder: allow → challenge → step-up → freeze & alert the fraud desk.
  • Separates "is it you?" from "is this safe?" to catch coercion and "safe account" scams.
  • Deepfake-resistant liveness: 4 random challenges per run, processed entirely in-browser.
  • Threat Theater: replay real Nigerian scam patterns against the live engine.
Python NiceGUI (FastAPI) MediaPipe Google Gemini MongoDB Atlas Railway
